Unit 2 · Level 4 · The Olympiad

The Welwitschia's Age

Two challenges — growth, in reverse

Challenge 1 · the puzzle 
# The Welwitschia mirabilis is one of the slowest-growing plants
# on Earth — some specimens out in the Namib are over 1500 years
# old. A botanist is tracking a seedling that's currently H cm
# tall. Each year it grows by G% of its CURRENT height, rounded
# down to the nearest whole centimetre.
#
# Given its starting height H, its growth rate G, and a number
# of years Y, what will its height be after Y years?
#
# Input: 100 10 3   Output: 133
✎ Working it out

Growth here is compounding — each year's growth is a percentage of the height after the previous year's growth, not the original height. So you can't just calculate one year and multiply by Y; you need to update the height Y times, once per year.

For H = 100, G = 10, Y = 3: year 1 grows by 100 × 10 ÷ 100 = 10 → height 110. Year 2 grows by 110 × 10 ÷ 100 = 11 → height 121. Year 3 grows by 121 × 10 ÷ 100 = 12 (rounded down from 12.1) → height 133.

1 Challenge 1 — Warm-up

Write a program that reads the starting height H, the growth rate G, and the number of years Y, then prints the plant's height after Y years. Use integer division so the height is always a whole number of centimetres.

▸ Need a hint?

Use a for loop that runs Y times. On each pass, compute growth = height * rate // 100, then add it on: height = height + growth. Print height once the loop finishes — not inside it.

▸ Show solution
height, rate, years = map(int, input().split())

for i in range(years):
    growth = height * rate // 100
    height = height + growth

print(height)
Try these H/G/Y combinations and check your output matches:
1.a  H=100, G=10, Y=3  → 133
1.b  H=50, G=20, Y=4  → 103
1.c  H=200, G=5, Y=0  → 200
1.d  H=10, G=50, Y=5  → 75
Challenge 2 · the puzzle 
# The botanist wants the reverse question answered: given the
# SAME seedling's starting height H and its growth rate G%, how
# many FULL years will it take before the plant's height is at
# least a target height T?
#
# Input: 50 20 100   Output: 4
2 Challenge 2 — No Solution Given

Write a program that reads the starting height H, the growth rate G, and a target height T (on one line, space-separated, or on separate lines — your choice), and prints the number of full years until the height reaches or exceeds T. If the plant has already reached T, the answer is 0.

This time you don't know the number of years in advance, so a fixed-count for loop won't work — use a while loop instead. Keep a year counter starting at 0. While height < target, apply one year's growth and add 1 to the counter. As soon as the condition is false, stop and print the counter.

Try these H/G/T combinations and check your output matches:
2.a  H=100, G=10, T=200  → 8
2.b  H=50, G=20, T=100  → 4
2.c  H=10, G=50, T=50  → 5
2.d  H=200, G=10, T=200 → 0
